I'm a game programmer with experience developing gameplay systems, tools, and interactive experiences across multiple programming languages and game engines.

I am a game designer and programmer passionate about creating engaging, interactive experiences. I have developed a variety of projects in Unity and Minecraft, with my story-driven puzzle adventure maps reaching more than 25,000 players worldwide. I enjoy combining programming and creative design to build immersive gameplay, intricate systems, and memorable player experiences.
